Privacy

Blit has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. What you transfer, where from and where to stays on your Mac.

  • Your servers and drives. Blit connects only to the SFTP servers and NAS devices you add, and to drives on your Mac. Passwords are stored in your Mac's Keychain; SSH keys are read from the file you choose each time Blit connects, and never copied.
  • Watch mode and download status. Using the login you added, Blit reads your download client's records on your server (qBittorrent's: each torrent's name, progress and sharing figures) and, for a download in progress, how much of it is on disk, with one command. None of it is sent anywhere else.
  • Notifications. The still in a download's notification is made on your Mac from the file itself. The welcome-back summary is made on your Mac from your own transfers.
  • Updates. Once a day, Blit checks blit.banast.as/appcast.xml for a newer version (turn this off in Settings › Updates). The request includes Blit's version number, and updates are signed so only genuine ones install.
  • Transfer history. The transfer log (file names, sizes, SHA-256 hashes, sources and destinations) is kept only on your Mac, in its Application Support folder.
  • Payments. If you choose to pay, Lemon Squeezy collects your payment and billing details as merchant of record, under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email address and order details, which we use to send receipts and to answer support requests. We don't sell or share them.
  • This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or trackers. It's hosted on Cloudflare, which processes requests to deliver the pages. When you click Pay what you want, the checkout opens from Lemon Squeezy (their script loads only then), and Lemon Squeezy may set its own cookies to run the checkout.
