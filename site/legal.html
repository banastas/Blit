Privacy

Blit has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. What you transfer, where from and where to stays on your Mac.

  • Your servers and drives. Blit connects only to the SFTP servers and NAS devices you add, and to drives on your Mac. Passwords are stored in your Mac's Keychain; SSH keys are read from the file you choose each time Blit connects, and never copied.
  • Updates. Once a day, Blit checks blit.banast.as/appcast.xml for a newer version (turn this off in Settings › Updates). The request includes Blit's version number, and updates are signed so only genuine ones install.
  • Transfer history. The transfer log (file names, sizes, SHA-256 hashes, sources and destinations) is kept only on your Mac, in its Application Support folder.
  • Licensing. To activate a license, Blit sends your license key and your Mac's name to Lemon Squeezy's license service (api.lemonsqueezy.com), and checks the key there about once a week. Lemon Squeezy returns whether the key is valid and the name and email it was bought with, which Blit shows in Settings.
